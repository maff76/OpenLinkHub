What changed
------------
1. Added one canonical authored-colour resolver for imported iCUE effects.
2. WaveLighting, RippleLighting and GradientLighting now expose the complete ordered transition palette instead of collapsing to the first transition colour in the editor/decoded model.
3. RainLighting, ColorWaveLighting, ColorShiftLighting, ColorPulseLighting and SequentialLighting use the same first/second colour decoder as playback.
4. Static/single-colour effects continue to use the renderer's transition-aware layerColor decoder.
5. RawParameters/AuthoredParameters remain untouched and available alongside decoded colours.
6. QX logical numbering and measured physical topology are unchanged.
7. DRAM keeps the z7d9j compatibility-family resolver (including Dram1030 handling).
8. Profile activation/lifecycle is unchanged.
9. Rain direction=4 is intentionally not guessed in this build; its semantics still need capture-based proof.
